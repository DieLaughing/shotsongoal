# What's new in Shots on Goal

Every update to [Shots on Goal](https://github.com/DieLaughing/shotsongoal), newest first. The same list is on the app's What's new page.
This file is generated from `data/changes.yaml` by `tools/changelog.py`; edit that file, not this one.

## November 2, 2026

- **New: The live dock: your team's score, everywhere.** While one of your teams is playing, the score now stays on screen above the tab bar on every page, so you can read the Wire or make your picks without losing it. It lights up when anyone scores and takes you to the game when you tap it. Two of your teams on at once? Swipe between them. Spoiler-free games stay out of it. Rather not have it? Turn off Show the live dock in Settings > Display and it's hidden on all your devices.
- **New: Game story.** Open a finished game and tap ▶ Game story to play it out one moment at a time, with the score and the rink catching up as you swipe. It's made for spoiler-free too. A game you're hiding now offers ▶ Catch up, goal by goal, and nothing shows the result until you get there, not even how many goals are left.
- **New: This just happened.** Tonight now opens with a row of your teams' latest goals, each one ready to watch, followed by the finals from last night and today. Spoiler-free games stay out of it.
- **New: The whole season at a glance.** Team pages now show every game of the season on one chart, wins above the line and losses below, bigger bars for bigger margins. Drag along it to see any game and the record at that point, then tap to open it.
- **New: Compare any two.** Put two players or two teams side by side, with who's ahead on each number and how their games this season went. Tap ⇄ Compare on a team or player page, or just search "Bruins vs Leafs".
- **New: A goal horn, if you want one.** You can now have a horn sound on your device when one of your teams scores while the app is open. It's off unless you turn it on, in Settings > Display, and it's set for each device separately.
- **Improved: Bigger headings.** Page titles now use the same typeface as the jumbotron, so the whole app looks a bit more like a night at the rink.

## November 1, 2026

- **New: Recaps, and your team's colours.** Finished games now have a 3-minute recap and the condensed game, playing right in the app (they turn up a while after the horn). Team pages wear the team's colours with their points up big, and player pages put the jersey number front and centre. The whole app now dresses in your team's colours too. If you follow several teams, your first favourite in each league leads, so the app can be Bruins gold on NHL and Fleet green on PWHL. To keep one team all the time, or turn it off, go to Settings › Display › Team colours.
- **New: Pick'em streaks and a countdown.** Pick'em now shows how long until your next unpicked game locks, once it's within 3 hours. It's the real puck-drop time, nothing invented. Three right in a row earns a 🔥 next to your name, and your best run and ⭐ perfect nights (at least 3 picks, all right) sit above the tables.
- **New: Easier first time in.** New friends now set up one question at a time (their teams, where they live, what they watch on) instead of hunting through Settings. Already set up? You can run it again from Settings › Your teams.
- **New: Goal clips play right here.** Tap ▶ Watch on a goal and the clip plays over the page in the NHL's own player, so there's no second page to open and nothing to find your way back from. Next goal steps through the rest of the game's goals, and Replay offers the same button when it stops on a goal. The NHL's player runs its usual ads. Close it and you're exactly where you were.
- **New: The arena.** Games got a lot more game. Every game page opens on a jumbotron in both teams' colours, and the score stays on screen as you scroll. When a goal goes in while you're watching, the number rolls up and the red goal lamp sweeps the board (tap a score on the jumbotron to light it yourself). Under the shot map, drag the puck through the game and watch it fill in shot by shot, or press play to see the whole game in about 20 seconds. On Pick'em, tap Flick your picks to go one game at a time and flick the puck at your team. On League, tap What if? and pick tonight's winners to watch the standings shuffle; nothing is saved, it's just for fun. Animations off in Settings › Display, or Reduce Motion on your phone, turns the moving parts off.
- **Improved: Opens faster.** The app now asks for what it needs at the very start instead of one thing after another, so a first open on a slow phone connection is about a quarter faster.

## October 28, 2026

- **New: College Pick'em.** Pick'em now has college games too. Tap NCAA at the top for just college, or All for everything. College has its own table, so it never mixes with the NHL and PWHL standings (switch between the two under the games), and its own ★ lock each night, so you don't have to choose between the Bruins and a college game. College picks are 1 point each (there are no odds for college, so no underdog bonus), and a tie counts as a wrong pick. Too many to bother with? Tap 🎲 Pick for me and it fills in every game you haven't picked yet (leaning on the odds where there are any). It never changes a pick you made or your ★, and a league you've folded away is left alone.
- **New: College hockey.** Men's and women's college hockey is here. Tap NCAA at the top for every college game, or All for everything at once. Tap a college league's heading to fold its games away on a busy night, and it stays the way you left it (under All it starts folded, so the NHL isn't buried). Pick your schools in Settings › Your teams, grouped by conference, and they get the same cards on Tonight, alerts for puck drop, goals and finals, calendar feeds and spoiler-free. Games show poll ranks, goals by period and where each one streams (ESPN+, B1G+, FloHockey, NCHC.tv…; tick the ones you have in Settings). The League tab has each conference's records, and team pages show the season at a glance (home, away and conference records, goals a game, last five, poll moves, the arena), the roster where ESPN has one, and game highlight videos. ESPN doesn't publish scorers or shots for college games, so game pages link to ESPN's box score for those.

## October 23, 2026

- **Fixed: The top and bottom bars sit right on iPhone.** In the app saved to an iPhone home screen, the top bar no longer fades out under the Dynamic Island, and the clock now sits on a solid strip that matches the app. On Tonight, the bottom bar sits flush with the bottom of the screen again. If you saved the app before today, delete its icon and add it to your home screen again to get the new top.
- **Improved: Games grow out of their cards.** Tap a game on Tonight and its card grows into the scoreboard at the top of the game page; go back and it shrinks back into place. Works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox; anywhere else the page just opens as before.
- **Improved: Smoother moves around the app.** Pages now build in from the top when you open them, and the highlight on the tab bar and on every switch (Wire filters, NHL / PWHL / Both, standings views, team and player tabs) stretches across to what you picked. Prefer the old slide-in? Settings › Display › Slapshot.
- **Improved: Search forgives typos.** Search now knows what fans call teams (Habs, B's, Bolts, Pens…), ignores accents and apostrophes, and still finds players when a name is misspelled, like "pastranak" or "mcdavis".
- **Improved: Live games stay put while they update.** During live games, Tonight and the game page now change only the scores, clocks and stats that moved every 10 seconds, instead of redrawing the whole page. Your team cards stay on the team you swiped to, the shot map keeps the period you picked, an open reaction picker stays open, and the player tables keep their place when you've scrolled them sideways.

## October 19, 2026

- **Improved: Upsets get their own column in Pick'em.** The ⚡ number in the Pick'em tables was easy to read as a streak. It counts your upsets, right picks on an underdog that won, and now sits in its own Upsets column.
- **Improved: Pages stay quick after a pick.** Making a pick, reacting to a goal or changing a setting used to make every other page reload from scratch the next time you opened it. Now only what actually changed reloads (a pick refreshes Pick'em, new favourite teams refresh the Wire), so the Wire, Leaders, standings and team pages still open instantly on a slow connection.

## October 18, 2026

- **New: Where to watch in Canada.** Watching from Canada? Settings → Where you watch now has a Country switch. Pick Canada, your home region (say, the Jets) and what you have (Sportsnet on TV, Sportsnet+ Standard or Premium, TSN, RDS, TVA Sports, Prime Video), and every game shows the right Canadian channel. That covers Sportsnet's national games, Monday Night Hockey on Sportsnet+, Wednesday Night Hockey on Prime, and your team's regional games on TSN, RDS or Sportsnet. Games from other regions, and US-only matchups, show as Sportsnet+ Premium. Thanks to whoever sent the 🇨🇦📺🤌 report.

## October 17, 2026

- **New: Player pages, leaders and search.** Tap any player's name (goal scorers, assists, three stars, box scores, rosters, team leaders) for their own page. It has their photo, age, size and draft spot, this season's numbers, a chart and log of their last games, their whole career season by season, and awards. NHL players also get an Advanced tab with expected goals and shot share from MoneyPuck.com, showing how they rank against the league. The League tab now has Leaders (points, goals, assists, wins, save % and GAA, for the NHL and PWHL), and the magnifying glass at the top searches every player and team. Team pages are tidier too, with the next game up top and then Games, Players and News tabs, with the roster and this season's numbers in one table. Teams now show their own logos. Prefer the old coloured badges? Settings → Display has a switch, and player photos can be turned off there too.

## October 16, 2026

- **Improved: Counting which pages get used.** The app now keeps a simple tally of how often each kind of page is opened (Tonight, game pages, Pick'em, the Wire and so on). It isn't tracking you. It never records who opened what, so not even the person running the app can see who clicked anything, just totals like "game pages were opened 40 times this week". It's strictly for deciding what to build and improve next. Tallies are kept for six months.

## October 15, 2026

- **Improved: Game previews before puck drop.** Open any upcoming NHL game for the matchup. Each team's record, division place, streak and recent results; how they compare this season (goals for and against, power play, penalty kill, faceoffs, with league ranks); who's hot over the last 5 games; each team's goalies this season; and the season series so far. It all steps aside once the game starts.
- **New: Odds, just for fun.** Every NHL game now shows who's favoured to win and their chance (from DraftKings' line, with the bookmaker's cut taken out), plus a note when the books expect a high- or low-scoring game. Open a game for the full picture, and after the final you'll see if it was an upset. Nothing links to betting, and Settings → Display → Show odds turns it off. In Pick'em, picking the underdog now pays double if they win (marked 2× before puck drop), and your lock doubles that again. The bonus starts with tonight's games.

## October 14, 2026

- **Improved: Tidier game page, and buttons under your thumb.** The top of a game page is now in clear groups. The score card is just the game (a power play or 4 on 4 shows under the clock), then where to watch it with one button per app, then you and your friends, with "I'm watching" and "Watching later" side by side. On a phone, foldable or tablet the buttons sit on your thumb's side. Left-handed? Settings → Display has a Right-handed / Left-handed switch. On a computer they're centred.
- **Fixed: Livelier game clock.** Left a game or Tonight open during play and the clock seemed stuck? On busy nights the scoreboard was waiting behind other background work. It now updates on its own steady beat, and open live pages check every 10 seconds, so the clock and score keep up with the NHL's own scoreboard (which itself moves about every 20 seconds).

## October 13, 2026

- **Improved: Clearer buttons and a Back button.** The tabs at the bottom now have icons so they look like the buttons they are (they tuck away while you scroll down, along with the top bar, so the page gets the whole screen, and come back as soon as you scroll up), Settings is a proper button with a gear, and the NHL / PWHL / Both switch shows which one is on more clearly. Game, team and other pages now have a Back button at the top that takes you to wherever you just were.
- **New: Page animations.** Moving around the app now has a little snap to it. A new page comes in like a slapshot, fast from the side, then hits the back of the net and settles, and the highlight in the tab bar slides to the tab you picked. Tabs come from their side of the bar, games slide in from the right and back out to the left. It's over in under a third of a second and only starts once the page is ready. Prefer instant? Settings → Display → Animations between pages turns it off (it's also off when your phone's Reduce Motion setting is on).
- **Fixed: Team cards side by side.** Following more than one team on a computer or tablet? Their cards now sit side by side instead of the second one hiding off the edge. On a phone you still swipe between them, with dots underneath showing there's more than one. Behind the scenes the app also does less work per page, so the Wire and League tabs load a little lighter.
- **Improved: Instant tabs.** After the app opens, it quietly loads your other tabs and your teams' pages, so switching tabs is instant even on a slow connection, and each page updates itself a moment later if anything changed. Behind the scenes, every team page and every game on tonight's slate is kept fresh whether or not anyone is looking, with your favorite teams first, so opening a game you haven't looked at yet doesn't wait either.
- **New: Milestones, momentum and fight alerts.** Your team card and team pages now show players close to a round career number ("Mittelstadt is 2 goals from 100"), and when someone gets there, the goal alert says so. Game pages have a Momentum chart (who's been getting the shots, every 5 minutes) with a shot-quality number for each team. And if you like that sort of thing, Settings → Alerts has a new Fights switch (off unless you turn it on). Pages also download less, so they open quicker on mobile data.
- **Fixed: Faster tabs again.** Switching tabs had slowed to a second or more, mostly for anyone not on the home network. Pages now show the latest saved data straight away and refresh it behind the scenes, the team cards load less, and spoiler-free checks no longer hold pages up. Most tabs should feel instant again.
- **Improved: A tidier app.** Less scrolling, same features. The tabs are now Tonight, Pick'em, Wire and League (standings plus your teams; tap any team for its page). Tonight doesn't list your teams' games twice, game cards are shorter (tickets and the blackout details live on the game page), and only one tip shows at a time. Game pages lead with the scoring and shot map, with team stats, penalties and players under "More stats". Settings is grouped into You, Alerts & spoilers, and More, with What's new right at the top.
- **New: Watch with friends.** Open a game and tap "👀 I'm watching" so friends can see you're on it. Who's watching shows on Tonight too. Once the puck drops, react to goals and penalties with 🔥 😭 🤬 🍺 🥅 and see what everyone else thought. When your team plays a friend's, the game page calls out the rivalry, and the final-score alert tells you who to gloat at (or who'll be gloating). Reactions are cleared at the end of each season.
- **New: Pick'em.** A new Pick'em tab. Tap who you think wins each game, NHL and PWHL, before puck drop. Star one game a night as your lock for 2 points if you're right (nothing if you're wrong). Friends' picks appear once a game starts, and the tables show who's leading this week and this season. Pick the name friends see in Settings → Pick'em.
- **New: Spoiler-free mode.** Watching on DVR or catching the game later? Open any game and tap "🙈 Watching later", or switch it on for a whole team in Settings → Spoiler-free. The score stays hidden on every page, goal and final alerts hold off, and the standings and Wire stories that could give it away are covered too, until you tap "I've seen it". Puck-drop alerts still come, and it switches off by itself the day after.
- **New: Your team, up top.** Tonight now opens with a card for each of your favorite teams, in their colors. It shows tonight's game (or the next one) and where to watch it, the live score once it starts, where they sit in the standings, how far they are from a playoff spot, any streak, the last 7 days and the next few games. Follow more than one team? Swipe sideways.
- **New: Report a problem.** Found something off, or have an idea? Tap "Something wrong? Report it" at the bottom of any page (or Settings → Report a problem). It goes straight onto the app's to-do list on GitHub without your name or email, along with which page you were on and what phone you're using, so there's no need to explain that part. Settings → Your reports shows whether it's been fixed, plus any reply.
- **Fixed: Opens on today's games.** In the morning, Tonight could still show last night's games, because the NHL's own feed doesn't switch to the new day until around midday. The app now switches by itself (last night's games stay up until 6 am Eastern, or until a late game finishes), and it refreshes when you come back to it after a while instead of showing the page you left.
